## Overview

Binance Adaptive Futures AI is an independent research project exploring AI-assisted cryptocurrency market analysis, multi-horizon forecasting, systematic prediction verification and risk-aware evaluation.

The platform is designed to analyse multiple digital-asset markets and generate research forecasts across different time horizons. Its objective is to evaluate forecasting consistency and reliability through recorded predictions and subsequent outcome verification.

The project is independently developed and is currently an experimental research prototype.

## Current Research Scope

| Component | Current scope |
|---|---|
| Markets | 10 cryptocurrency markets |
| Engineered features | 36 |
| Forecast horizons | 1h, 3h, 6h, 12h and 24h |
| Forecast coverage | 50 forecasts per complete cycle |
| Research signals | BUY / SELL / HOLD |
| Evaluation | Prediction logging and outcome verification |
| Operating mode | Observation-only |

Markets currently covered:

BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, DOGE, LINK, ADA, AVAX and LTC.

## Research Workflow

1. Collect cryptocurrency market data.
2. Process and engineer market features.
3. Generate forecasts across multiple time horizons.
4. Produce research signals for manual review.
5. Record predictions and evaluate them against subsequent market outcomes.
6. Analyse forecasting performance and risk-related limitations.

## BNB Chain Roadmap

BNB Chain integration is a proposed future development area, not an existing deployment.

Areas being investigated include:

- Feasibility of timestamped and verifiable prediction records.
- Cryptographic hashes for research-record integrity.
- Potential smart-contract attestations.
- Possible use of BNB Greenfield for decentralized research-data storage.
- Security and architecture requirements for any future integration.

Any implementation would follow technical feasibility assessment and appropriate security review.
