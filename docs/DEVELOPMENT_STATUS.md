## 3. Research Coverage

The current research scope includes:

- BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, DOGE, LINK, ADA, AVAX and LTC.
- 36 engineered features.
- Forecast horizons of 1h, 3h, 6h, 12h and 24h.
- Up to 50 forecasts per complete cycle.

## 4. Operational Safety

The development approach prioritizes controlled observation and manual review.

- Automated real-money trading: Disabled.
- Exchange order submission: Disabled.
- Research signals: Subject to manual review.
- Profitability: Not established.
- Forecast reliability: Requires further validation.

## 5. Ongoing Work

Current research priorities include:

- Forecasting robustness and out-of-sample validation.
- Prediction accuracy and error analysis.
- Evaluation across different market conditions.
- Realistic transaction-cost assessment.
- Improved prediction-record integrity.
- Better technical documentation.

## 6. Proposed Future Development

The following are research proposals, not completed integrations:

- BNB Chain smart-contract attestations.
- BNB Greenfield storage evaluation.
- Cryptographic prediction-record verification.
- Public verification interface.
- Further infrastructure and security improvements.

## 7. Current Limitations

The project remains experimental. Forecast generation does not establish consistent predictive accuracy or net trading profitability.

The project has no currently deployed BNB Chain smart contracts, token, or on-chain user base.

No commercial revenue or customer adoption is claimed.

## 8. Development Approach

The project is independently developed by a solo founder.

Development priorities are technical reliability, transparent evaluation, security, responsible experimentation and a carefully assessed future ecosystem integration.
