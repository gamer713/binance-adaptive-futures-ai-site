# Project Overview

## 1. Project Introduction

Binance Adaptive Futures AI is an independent, AI-powered digital-asset market research and forecasting project developed by a solo founder.

The project explores multi-market, multi-horizon cryptocurrency forecasting, systematic prediction recording, outcome verification and risk-aware evaluation.

Its objective is to develop a research platform that evaluates forecast quality transparently rather than assuming that generated predictions are accurate or profitable.

## 2. Problem Statement

Digital-asset markets operate continuously and can exhibit rapid changes in volatility, direction and market conditions.

Analysing several markets across multiple time horizons creates challenges in consistency, prediction tracking and performance evaluation.

This project investigates how an AI-assisted research workflow can combine market-data processing, engineered features, forecasting and systematic verification.

## 3. Current Project Scope

The current research universe covers 10 cryptocurrency markets:

BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, DOGE, LINK, ADA, AVAX and LTC.

The platform currently uses:

- 36 engineered features.
- Five forecast horizons: 1h, 3h, 6h, 12h and 24h.
- Up to 50 forecasts per complete cycle.
- BUY, SELL and HOLD research signals.
- Prediction recording and subsequent outcome verification.

## 4. Core Objectives

1. Develop multi-market cryptocurrency forecasting capabilities.
2. Generate predictions across multiple time horizons.
3. Record forecasts for systematic evaluation.
4. Verify predictions against subsequent market outcomes.
5. Investigate forecasting reliability and risk-related limitations.
6. Explore potential future verifiable research infrastructure.

## 5. Current Development Stage

The project is an early-stage experimental research prototype.

Forecast generation and prediction-verification workflows have been developed, while further robustness, out-of-sample and realistic trading-cost evaluation remains ongoing.

The project does not claim validated net trading profitability.

## 6. Operational Safety

The current system operates in observation-only mode.

- Automated real-money trading is disabled.
- Exchange order submission is disabled.
- BUY, SELL and HOLD outputs are research signals for manual review.
- No guaranteed trading results are claimed.

## 7. Proposed BNB Chain Exploration

A future development objective is to investigate how BNB Chain ecosystem technologies could support research-record integrity and verifiability.

Potential areas include:

- Cryptographic hashes of prediction records.
- Timestamped research attestations.
- Smart-contract-based verification.
- Evaluation of BNB Greenfield for decentralized storage.

These are proposed research directions. No BNB Chain smart contracts, on-chain prediction records or Greenfield integration are currently deployed.
