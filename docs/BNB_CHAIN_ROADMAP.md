# Proposed BNB Chain Integration Roadmap

## 1. Vision

Binance Adaptive Futures AI is an independent AI-powered digital-asset market research and forecasting project.

A future development objective is to investigate whether BNB Chain ecosystem technologies can improve the integrity, traceability and verifiability of AI-generated research records.

BNB Chain integration is a proposed direction. The current forecasting platform is not deployed on BNB Chain.

## 2. Why Explore BNB Chain?

The project generates predictions across multiple markets and time horizons.

A verifiable record system could potentially help establish:

- When a research prediction was created.
- Which model or system version generated it.
- Whether a stored prediction record has been altered.
- How the original prediction relates to its subsequent evaluation.

The objective is research transparency, not automatic trading or speculative token issuance.

## 3. Proposed Integration Areas

### A. Prediction Record Integrity

Investigate generating cryptographic hashes of prediction records.

The purpose would be to make later modifications detectable while keeping sensitive or detailed research data off-chain.

### B. Timestamped Attestations

Explore whether BNB Chain smart contracts could provide verifiable references to prediction-record hashes and associated timestamps.

### C. Decentralized Data Storage

Evaluate whether BNB Greenfield could be suitable for storing selected research artefacts, evaluation reports or public documentation.

### D. Verification Interface

Investigate a future interface through which an independent reviewer could verify the integrity of published research records.

## 4. Proposed Development Phases

### Phase 1 — Technical Feasibility

- Study relevant BNB Chain infrastructure.
- Evaluate smart-contract requirements.
- Assess BNB Greenfield storage suitability.
- Estimate development and operating costs.
- Identify security and privacy considerations.

### Phase 2 — Architecture and Prototype Design

- Define a prediction-record format.
- Design cryptographic integrity checks.
- Define model-version and timestamp metadata.
- Develop a test architecture.

### Phase 3 — Controlled Testnet Experiment

Subject to feasibility and security review:

- Experiment with a limited smart-contract attestation.
- Test record-hash verification.
- Evaluate storage and retrieval.
- Measure performance and costs.

### Phase 4 — Independent Review

- Review the architecture.
- Assess smart-contract security.
- Validate record-integrity assumptions.
- Document limitations and operational requirements.

Any mainnet deployment would require separate assessment and approval.

## 5. What Is Not Yet Implemented

The following are proposed future capabilities, not current features:

- BNB Chain smart-contract deployment.
- On-chain prediction attestations.
- BNB Greenfield integration.
- Public on-chain verification interface.
- Tokens or token-based incentives.

As an independent solo founder, I am seeking:

- BNB Chain technical mentorship.

- Security review recommendations.

## 7. Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective is to explore a transparent and independently verifiable AI research platform in which forecasting records can be evaluated against subsequent real-world market outcomes.

The project will prioritize technical feasibility, research integrity, responsible development and security before considering production deployment.
