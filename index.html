01 / Project overview

Making market forecasts measurable and reviewable

The project investigates how AI-assisted market forecasting can support structured digital-asset research. Its central objective is to move beyond isolated predictions by recording forecasts, measuring subsequent outcomes and evaluating model behaviour under changing market conditions.

04 / Development roadmap

Milestones with measurable deliverables

The roadmap prioritizes validation before deployment and security before public blockchain use.

PHASE 1 · CURRENT FOUNDATION

Research prototype

Maintain the multi-market forecasting pipeline, prediction records and outcome-verification workflow.

PHASE 2 · VALIDATION

Model evaluation

Expand out-of-sample testing, data-quality checks, forecast-error analysis and cost-aware risk evaluation.

PHASE 3 · WEB3 DESIGN

BNB Chain architecture

Define a minimal verification use case, data model, privacy boundaries and infrastructure requirements.

PHASE 4 · PROTOTYPE

Controlled testnet work

If justified, build and test a limited BNB Chain prototype with documented test cases.

PHASE 5 · SECURITY

Independent review

Assess smart-contract security, operational controls, data integrity and deployment readiness.

PHASE 6 · FUTURE

Ecosystem readiness

Evaluate public release feasibility, documentation, user needs and sustainable infrastructure.

06 / Responsible development

Human oversight, transparent limitations and risk awareness

Operational safeguards

  • Automated exchange order submission disabled.
  • Research signals intended for manual review.
  • Prediction outcomes require subsequent verification.
  • Model performance must be evaluated out of sample.
  • Transaction costs and market risks must be considered.

Research limitations

  • Forecasts can be incorrect.
  • Historical observations do not guarantee future results.
  • Slippage, spread and funding can affect outcomes.
  • Blockchain integration remains a proposed development.
  • No investment return or profitability is promised.

This is experimental research software, not financial advice or a guaranteed trading system. It is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Binance.
